Destination Overview

Xi'an — Where the Silk Road Began

Xi'an is one of humanity's most historic cities — the starting point of the ancient Silk Road, capital of 13 Chinese dynasties, and home to the most astonishing archaeological discovery of the 20th century: the Terracotta Army of Emperor Qin Shi Huang. But Xi'an is also vibrantly alive today — its Ming Dynasty city walls still intact, its Muslim Quarter fragrant with cumin and chilli, its night markets blazing under lanterns. Your BWC companion brings 3,000 years of history to life.

Day by Day

Your 5-Day Xi'an Itinerary

01
Day One
Arrival — City Wall Cycling & Muslim Quarter Food Crawl
Your BWC companion meets you at Xi'an Xianyang International Airport and transfers you to your hotel within the ancient city walls. The afternoon begins with the iconic Ming Dynasty City Wall — 13.7km of intact battlements that still encircle the old city. You'll cycle the full perimeter as the sun drops golden over the tiled rooftops below, watching Xi'an shift from day to evening light. As dusk deepens, your companion leads you into the Muslim Quarter: a labyrinth of alley stalls selling biang biang noodles slapped broad as a belt, lamb skewers charred over charcoal, crispy sesame flatbreads, and cold pomegranate juice squeezed fresh. One of China's greatest street food experiences.

02
Day Two
Terracotta Warriors — Full-Day VIP Access to All Three Pits
A full day at the greatest archaeological site on Earth. Your BWC companion arranges skip-the-queue VIP entry to all three pits of the Mausoleum of Emperor Qin Shi Huang — UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the Seven Wonders of the Modern World. Pit 1 alone contains over 6,000 life-size warriors in battle formation, each with a unique face, originally painted in vivid colour. Pit 2 reveals cavalry and archers; Pit 3 the command headquarters. Your private expert guide brings the Emperor's story to life: a megalomaniac genius who unified China, built the Great Wall, and was buried with an entire terracotta army — plus his 70,000 workers. Afternoon: Han Yang Mausoleum and its miniaturised terracotta figures.
UNESCOWarriorsHistory
03
Day Three
Shaanxi History Museum, Wild Goose Pagoda & Datang Night Show
Morning at the Shaanxi History Museum — one of China's finest, housing the rarest Tang Dynasty gold and silver artifacts, ancient silk textiles, painted tomb murals, and jade burial suits. Your companion navigates the collection with scholarly insight, illuminating China's extraordinary 5,000-year civilisation arc. Next, the Big Wild Goose Pagoda (Dayan Ta) — a 7th-century Tang Dynasty Buddhist tower built to house scriptures brought from India by the monk Xuanzang, whose journey inspired the classic novel Journey to the West. As evening falls, the Datang Everbright City pedestrian precinct stages a spectacular outdoor historical light and water show, re-enacting the glory of Tang Dynasty Chang'an.
MuseumPagodaNight Show
04
Day Four
Day Trip — Huashan Mountain, Cable Car & Plank Walk Adventure
One of China's Five Sacred Mountains awaits: Huashan (Hua Mountain), rising to 2,154m with sheer granite cliffs that have inspired artists and pilgrims for millennia. Your BWC companion accompanies you on the cable car ascent to the North Peak, then onward through the dramatic series of ridgelines — South, East, West, and Central peaks — connected by narrow stone staircases carved into vertical cliff faces. For the adventurous: the legendary Plank Walk on the South Peak — a narrow wooden board path bolted into a sheer cliff face 2,000 metres above the valley floor. Thrilling, unforgettable, and one of China's ultimate bucket list experiences.
MountainAdventureHiking
05
Day Five
Morning Calligraphy Class & Farewell Dumplings Banquet
Begin your final morning with a traditional Chinese calligraphy class — guided by a local master calligrapher, you'll learn to hold the brush correctly and write elegant characters on rice paper, taking home your work as a memento. Your farewell meal is the legendary dumplings banquet at De Fa Chang — Xi'an's most celebrated restaurant, which has served hundreds of varieties of jiaozi since 1936: dumplings shaped like goldfish, pomegranates, and chrysanthemums, each with a different fragrant filling. Your BWC companion accompanies you to hotel checkout and private transfer to the airport, ensuring a seamless farewell.
