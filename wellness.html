Our Approach

Your Trusted Companions in China

When you travel with BWC, you're not hiring a tour guide. You're being taken care of by people who live in China, know the culture, speak the language, and genuinely care about your experience.

We personally accompany each client — meaning you will always have us beside you, not just a printed itinerary. From the moment you land to the moment you depart, we are there to make sure everything goes smoothly.

Because we personally accompany our clients, each journey is limited to 1–3 guests per trip — ensuring you receive our full, undivided attention. Additional guests can be arranged with advance notice.
