Our Destinations

Six Extraordinary
Destinations in China

01 — Hainan
Hainan Island
China's tropical paradise. Azure beaches, luxury resorts in Sanya, and the Free Trade Port's modern energy — a world apart from the mainland.
Explore Hainan →
02 — Beijing
Beijing
The Forbidden City, the Great Wall, and ancient hutongs. China's imperial capital blends 5,000 years of history with world-class modern luxury.
Explore Beijing →
03 — Guilin
Guilin
Dramatic karst mountains rising from the Li River. One of China's most breathtaking landscapes, best experienced by private boat at sunrise.
Explore Guilin →
04 — Shanghai
Shanghai
The Bund's colonial grandeur facing Pudong's futuristic skyline. Shanghai is cosmopolitan, electric, and endlessly fascinating — China's global city.
Explore Shanghai →
05 — Xi'an
Xi'an
Home of the Terracotta Warriors and the ancient Silk Road. Walk the city walls, explore the Muslim Quarter, and step into the heart of ancient China.
Explore Xi'an →
06 — Xinjiang
Xinjiang
Ancient Silk Road cities, vast deserts, snow-capped peaks, and vibrant bazaars. Xinjiang is China's most dramatic and culturally rich frontier.
