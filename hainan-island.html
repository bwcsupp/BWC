Destination Highlights

Hainan — China's Hawaii

Hainan Island, at China's southernmost tip, is a world unto itself. Sanya's white-sand beaches rival the Maldives, while Haikou's vibrant city energy and the island's lush tropical interior offer endless variety. As China's Free Trade Port, Hainan also has the world's largest duty-free shopping complex.

Day by Day

Your 5-Day Hainan Itinerary

01
Day One
✈️ Arrival in Sanya · Check-in & First Impressions
Your BWC companion meets you at Sanya Phoenix International Airport with a welcome sign. Private transfer to your chosen hotel on Dadonghai Bay or Yalong Bay. After check-in, stroll the beachfront promenade as the sun sets over the South China Sea, with a welcome seafood dinner at a top beachside restaurant — your companion handles all reservations and communication.
Airport Pick-upPrivate TransferSeaside DinnerHotel Check-in
02
Day Two
🏖️ Yalong Bay · Snorkeling & Luxury Beach Day
Yalong Bay, consistently ranked among Asia's most beautiful beaches. Private boat trip for snorkeling over vibrant coral reefs. Lunch at the famous Yalong Bay seafood market where your companion negotiates the freshest catch at local prices. Afternoon at leisure on the beach. Optional: parasailing or banana boat with your companion for safety and booking support.
Yalong BaySnorkelingPrivate BoatSeafood Lunch
03
Day Three
🕉️ Nanshan Temple · Culture & Spirituality
Morning visit to the magnificent Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone, home of the world's tallest sea-based Guanyin statue (108 metres). Your companion provides fascinating insight into Buddhist culture and Chinese spirituality. Afternoon: Tianya Haijiao (Edge of the World), Hainan's most iconic landmark — the ancient inscribed boulders where legend says lovers made eternal vows. Sundowner cocktails at a cliffside bar in the evening.
Nanshan TempleGuanyin StatueTianya HaijiaoCultural Insights
04
Day Four
🛍️ Haitang Bay Duty-Free · World's Largest Duty-Free Complex
The CDF Mall at Haitang Bay — the world's largest duty-free shopping destination with 12 luxury department stores including Galeries Lafayette. Your companion helps navigate the vast complex, assists with purchases, and ensures you maximise the remarkable tax-free savings on luxury goods, cosmetics, jewellery, electronics, and fine spirits. Optional: Nanwan Monkey Island in the morning — a unique experience with thousands of Macaque monkeys in a tropical nature reserve.
CDF Duty-FreeLuxury ShoppingMonkey IslandTropical Nature
05
Day Five
🌅 Farewell Morning · Departure
Final sunrise beach walk or last-minute shopping. Your companion assists with luggage, hotel checkout, and arranges your private transfer to Sanya Airport (or onward to next destination). They remain with you until you pass through airport security, ensuring a smooth and memorable farewell to Hainan Island. Optional extension: continue your journey to another BWC destination.
