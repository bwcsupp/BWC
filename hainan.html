Setup Timeline

From Decision to
Ready to Operate

Our typical WFOE setup takes 6–10 weeks. Here is what to expect at each stage.

1

Initial Consultation & Planning

We assess your business model, recommend the right entity type and location, and outline the full cost and timeline.

Week 1
2

Document Preparation

We help prepare all required documents including notarized corporate docs, business plans, and articles of association.

Weeks 1–2
3

Name Approval & Registration

Company name pre-approval at the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR), followed by full registration filing.

Weeks 2–4
4

Business License Issuance

Official business license is granted. We coordinate the registered address, legal representative, and scope of business.

Weeks 4–6
5

Bank Account & Tax Registration

Corporate bank account opened (RMB + foreign currency), tax registration, and optional VAT general taxpayer status.

Weeks 6–10
6

Ready to Operate

Your company is fully operational. We offer ongoing compliance, accounting, and HR support if needed.

Requirements

What You'll
Need to Provide

We handle the complexity. You just need to provide these standard documents — we'll guide you on format and notarization requirements.

🪪

Passport Copy

Valid passport for all shareholders and the legal representative. Notarized and apostilled if required.

🏢

Parent Company Documents

Certificate of incorporation, articles of association, and board resolution for corporate shareholders.

📄

Business Plan

Brief description of intended operations, products or services, and expected revenue scope in China.

📍

Registered Address

A physical office address in China. We can provide a registered address if you don't have one yet.

💰

Registered Capital

Minimum registered capital varies by entity type and location. We advise on the optimal amount for your situation.
