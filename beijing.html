About This Journey

China's Imperial Heart

Beijing is one of the world's great imperial cities — 5,000 years of history written in stone, silk, and jade. From the vast Tiananmen Square to the labyrinthine Forbidden City, from the soaring Great Wall to the serene Summer Palace, every corner reveals a layer of China's extraordinary past. Your BWC companion ensures you experience it all with depth, comfort, and zero hassle.

Day by Day

Your 5-Day Itinerary

01
Day One
Arrival · Tiananmen Square & First Evening
Meet at Beijing Capital Airport. Private transfer to hotel in central Beijing. After settling in, evening stroll through Tiananmen Square — the largest public square in the world — and walk along Chang'an Avenue as the city lights up. Welcome dinner at a classic Beijing restaurant with your companion.
Airport TransferTiananmen SquareWelcome Dinner
02
Day Two
Forbidden City · Jingshan Park · Hutong Evening
Full morning in the Forbidden City (Palace Museum) — 980 buildings, 24 emperors, and centuries of imperial splendour. Your companion provides rich historical context at every hall. Climb Jingshan Park for the famous panoramic view over the entire Forbidden City. Evening rickshaw tour through Beijing's ancient hutong alleyways, ending at a private courtyard house for dinner.
Forbidden CityImperial HistoryHutong TourCourtyard Dinner
03
Day Three
Great Wall · Ming Tombs · Peking Duck
Early morning drive to the Mutianyu section of the Great Wall — less crowded, beautifully restored, with cable car access. Your companion arranges skip-the-line entry. Afternoon visit to the Ming Tombs (Changling). Return to Beijing for the ultimate Peking Duck dinner at Quanjude or Da Dong — the definitive Beijing experience.
Great WallMutianyuMing TombsPeking Duck Dinner
04
Day Four
Summer Palace · 798 Art District · Night Market
Morning at the magnificent Summer Palace — 290 hectares of imperial gardens, Kunming Lake, and the iconic Long Corridor with 14,000 painted scenes. Afternoon at the contemporary 798 Art District. Evening at Wangfujing Night Market — your companion helps you navigate the legendary street food stalls.
Summer PalaceImperial Gardens798 Art DistrictNight Market
05
Day Five
Temple of Heaven · Farewell & Departure
Morning visit to the Temple of Heaven — the most perfectly proportioned building in China, where emperors prayed for good harvests. Watch locals doing tai chi in the surrounding park. Final shopping opportunity at Silk Street or Panjiayuan Antique Market. Private transfer to airport with your companion by your side until check-in.
Temple of HeavenTai ChiShoppingDeparture Transfer
