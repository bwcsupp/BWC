Destination Overview

Beijing — Where History Lives

Beijing is one of the world's great imperial cities — 5,000 years of history written in stone, silk, and jade. From vast Tiananmen Square to the labyrinthine Forbidden City, from the soaring Great Wall to the serene Summer Palace, every corner reveals a layer of China's extraordinary civilisation. Your BWC companion brings it all to life with insight, humour, and seamless logistics.

Day by Day

Your 5-Day Beijing Itinerary

01
Day One
✈️ Arrival · Tiananmen Square & First Evening
Your BWC companion meets you at Beijing Capital Airport (or Daxing) with a welcome sign. Private transfer to your centrally located hotel in the Dongcheng or Xicheng district. After settling in, your evening begins with a stroll across Tiananmen Square — the world's largest public square — and a walk along Chang'an Avenue as the city lights illuminate the ancient gates. Welcome dinner at a classic Beijing restaurant, where your companion introduces you to northern Chinese cuisine: crispy spring rolls, braised pork, and hand-pulled noodles.
Airport TransferTiananmen SquareWelcome DinnerCentral Beijing
02
Day Two
🏯 Forbidden City · Jingshan · Hutong Rickshaw
A full morning inside the Palace Museum (Forbidden City) — 980 buildings, 24 emperors, and 600 years of imperial splendour. Your companion provides rich historical context at every hall, courtyard, and treasure gallery. Climb Jingshan Park (Coal Hill) for the iconic panoramic view over the entire Forbidden City — the best photo in Beijing. Evening rickshaw tour through Beijing's ancient hutong alleyways, winding through centuries-old courtyard houses (siheyuan). End with dinner at a private courtyard restaurant that dates back to the Qing Dynasty.
Forbidden CityImperial HistoryJingshan ParkHutong RickshawCourtyard Dinner
03
Day Three
🏔️ Great Wall · Ming Tombs · Peking Duck Dinner
Early morning drive to the Mutianyu section of the Great Wall — beautifully restored, far less crowded than Badaling, with a cable car that takes you up through the forested mountainside. Stand on one of humanity's greatest constructions as it snakes over ridgelines to the horizon. Your companion arranges skip-the-line entry. Afternoon: the Ming Tombs (Changling) — the burial site of 13 Ming emperors, with a perfectly preserved spirit way lined with stone animals and officials. Return to Beijing for the ultimate Peking Duck dinner at a renowned restaurant — your companion ensures the full ceremonial carving experience.
Great Wall MutianyuCable CarMing TombsPeking Duck
04
Day Four
🌸 Summer Palace · 798 Art District · Night Market
Morning at the magnificent Summer Palace (Yihe Yuan) — 290 hectares of imperial gardens, the vast Kunming Lake, the famous Long Corridor adorned with 14,000 painted scenes, and Longevity Hill with its glittering temples. An afternoon exploring Beijing's coolest neighbourhood: the 798 Art District — converted Bauhaus-era factories now housing world-class contemporary art galleries, studios, and cafés. Evening: Wangfujing Night Market, where your companion helps you navigate legendary street food stalls — scorpions optional.
Summer PalaceImperial Gardens798 Art DistrictNight Market
05
Day Five
🛕 Temple of Heaven · Final Moments · Departure
Morning visit to the Temple of Heaven (Tiantan) — widely considered the most perfectly proportioned building in China, where emperors offered prayers for good harvests. Watch locals performing tai chi and flying kites in the vast surrounding park. A quintessentially Beijing experience. Final shopping opportunity at Silk Street Market or Panjiayuan Antique Market for silks, jade, calligraphy, and traditional art. Your companion stays with you through luggage collection, hotel checkout, and private transfer to the airport — right up to the departure gate.
